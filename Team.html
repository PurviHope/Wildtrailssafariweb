Meet the Team

Nischit Solanky

Nischit is a semi-pro wildlife photographer with experience of more than 9 years. He proudly displays his work on Instagram@nischit_solanky His passion for wildlife is addictive and with WildTrails Safari he wants to create awareness of the beautiful world we live in, along with a very selfish pitch of saving the environment and the world for future generations. “If Wildlife is your game; Come play with us.”

Benjamin Mwithi

NBenjamin Mwithi is a professional safari guide who has a Degree in tourism and wildlife management from Moi university and silver level badge by Kenya professional safari guide association. He has an industry experience of more than 14 years;working with Serena hotels and safari lodges/ camps for several years as naturalist and safari guide. He worked with a few other well known Safari companies before opening his Own safari company called African Hats Safaris (since 2017). African Hats Safaris has teamed up with WildTrails Safari to plan bespoke safaris for clients from all corners of the world. They run a fleet of well-maintained luxury safari Landcruisers which are driven by well trained professional drivers/guides.

Purvi Hope

Purvi Hope is an entrepreneur who runs an Education company called Hope Education and a Designer company called Hope Studio, she has a heart for nature and is a partner in WildTrails Safari; she manages the India office. She supports various causes like Women empowerment and Nature conservation with her various associations and wants to make people aware of the world around us. She will be happy to help you with all your safari needs.

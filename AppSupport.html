FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Animal Detector work?

The Animal Detector uses AI technology to identify wildlife in your photos. Simply take a picture or choose one from your gallery, and our system will analyze it to detect animals and provide detailed information about them.

Can I use the app offline?

The Animal Detector requires an internet connection to process images through our AI system.

How does the Animal Detector work?

Our AI model is trained on extensive wildlife datasets and provides high accuracy. Confidence scores are shown for each detection to help you understand the reliability of the results. **Since our Ai continuously learns as it goes, there might be time when it could not recognize certain animals.

How do I book a safari through the app?

Currently, the app provides information about our signature safaris. To book, please contact us directly through the WhatsApp numbers or email provided in the support section.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we take your privacy seriously. Photos processed through Animal Detector are not stored on our servers after analysis, and we don't share your personal information with third parties.
